SLIDE: NLP Preprocessing Pipeline | 1 | Raw Input | User types a query. Raw text is captured as-is. | 2 | Tokenization | Text split into individual words (tokens) using NLTK word_tokenize. | 3 | Lowercasing | & Stemming | Tokens lowercased; stemmed to root form using PorterStemmer. | 4 | Stopword | Removal | Common words (is, the, a) filtered out to focus on meaningful tokens. | 5 | Bag of Words | Tokens converted to a numeric vector matching the vocabulary. | Each step refines the raw input into a clean, machine-readable format that powers accurate intent recognition.
